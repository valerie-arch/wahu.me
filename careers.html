Our values

What we stand for.

Five values that shape how we hire, how we build, and how we treat each other on hard days.

01

Caring passionately

We are committed to excellence and innovation, creating positive change beyond ourselves. We care deeply for each other, our customers, and the quality of our work.

02

Bold champions

We take calculated risks and bold actions to seize market opportunities and achieve commercial success.

03

Inclusive

We respect and embrace diversity.

04

Resourceful

We creatively leverage resources to overcome challenges. As problem solvers, we aren't scared to take the decision — and we do it quickly.

05

Trustworthy

We deliver on our promises with transparency and integrity, ensuring ethical conduct in all our actions.

Why Wahu

Four reasons people stay.

There are easier places to work. Here's what people who join Wahu tell us — once they've been around long enough to know.

01

The work is real.

Every line of code, every weld, every conversation with a rider has a person on the other end. You ship something on Monday and see it on the road on Friday. There is no theatre here.

02

A team of relentless learners.

Engineers from leading universities, designers from world-class studios, operators who've built businesses on three continents. Smart people, low ego, high standards. Iron sharpens iron.

03

We learn obsessively.

From AI and battery innovation to behavioural economics and field operations — we read, we tinker, we teach each other. Curiosity is how we hire, and how we keep getting better.

04

The mission is bigger than us.

This is one of the largest economic and environmental transitions of our generation. Africa is going electric — and we're not waiting for someone else to design what that looks like.
