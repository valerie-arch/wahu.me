The thesis

The winners won't be the people who shipped the most hardware.

Electric mobility in Africa is at the inflection point. The winners won't be the people who shipped the most hardware — they'll be the ones who own the data layer underneath it.

A bike is sold once. A subscription builds a relationship. Data unlocks scale.

The operating system

One operating system. Three layers.

Hardware on the road. Playbook in the software. Intelligence across both.

01 · Hardware

The vehicles, batteries, and charging that move Africa's last mile.

Designed and assembled in Accra. Built for road conditions, rider economics, and uptime no imported product solves for.

02 · Playbook

The software that runs the fleet.

Onboarding, payments, performance, collections, and path-to-ownership — codified into a system any operator can run, anywhere on the continent.

03 · Intelligence

The data and AI layer that learns from every rider, every kilometer, every payment.

Turning a fleet business into a compounding asset that gets smarter with scale.

The opportunity

The market is moving.

African EV market projected size, driven by two-wheelers, fleet electrification, and policy tailwinds.

25M

Roughly 25M motorbikes on African roads today, against 320M in India — same population. The gap is the opportunity.

22%
By 2030 · EV share

Two-wheelers expected to reach 22% electrification — well ahead of cars or any other category.

30%
Per km · Cheaper

Electric two-wheelers cost ~30% less per kilometer than petrol — and the gap widens as fuel prices rise.

Leadership

The team building it.

Valerie Labi
Co-founder · CEO

Founded Wahu in 2022 and opened Ghana's first electric vehicle manufacturing plant in Accra in 2024. Pioneered Wahu's authorization to sell carbon credits under Article 6 of the Paris Agreement — only the second e-mobility company globally to secure this, anchored by a landmark offtake with Switzerland. Twenty years across Vodafone, EY, and impact ventures in West Africa. MSt Sustainability Leadership, University of Cambridge. Mandela Washington Fellow. Harambean.

Abena Baidoo, FCCA
Chief Financial Officer

Joined as CFO in 2024. Owns finance, capital structure, and investor relations. Seventeen years across corporate finance, transactions, and sustainable finance — Senior Manager in Transaction Advisory at PwC Ghana, and Assistant Vice President in Corporate Finance at Databank. Built Wahu's unit economics, the six-stage financing strategy, and the Article 6.2 transaction architecture. FCCA. MBA (Distinction), Anglia Ruskin.

Engr. Christopher Amewuho
Head of Technology

Owns vehicle R&D and product improvement — battery systems, drivetrain, range, durability, and the engineering roadmap for new platforms. Previously CEO of SolarTaxi, where he led the build-out that seeded Ghana's EV sector, and CTO of DAK Innovations. Has designed lithium-ion battery systems for solar and mobility, retrofitted ICE motorbikes achieving 132 km range, and led an EV assembly line that lifted vehicle performance 400%. BSc Materials Engineering, KNUST. Professional Engineer, Ghana.
